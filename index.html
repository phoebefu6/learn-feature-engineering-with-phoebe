Learn Feature Engineering with Phoebe

Two tracks into the inputs that decide your model

A column is what the database gives you. A feature is what the model can learn from - and almost all of the winnable performance in applied machine learning lives in the gap between them. A leader track teaches why feature work beats model swaps, how a suspiciously good score is a red flag rather than a win, and what feature governance actually costs. A practitioner track rebuilds Lumen's checkout data column by column: scaling, encoding, datetime decomposition, interactions, missing-data signal, selection, and an honest bridge to embeddings. A live workbench trains a real model in your browser - watch held-out AUC climb 0.500 to 0.813 with the algorithm never changing, then press the leakage lever and watch a training score of 0.967 collapse to 0.789 the moment the column that knew the future goes missing.
